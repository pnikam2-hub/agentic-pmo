# Board Diversity & Diversity Policy — Domain Pack

Part of the **Agentic PMO** platform. This domain pack supports governance advisory
engagements focused on corporate board composition, board diversity and effectiveness,
and Diversity, Equity & Inclusion (DEI / EDI) policy design, implementation, and measurement.

## Domain context

Board diversity has moved from a "nice-to-have" governance aspiration to a regulated,
investor-scrutinised, and evidence-backed pillar of corporate governance. This pack covers
the full engagement lifecycle — from baseline discovery through benchmarking, policy design,
board adoption, implementation, and ongoing measurement — and is grounded in current UK and
international frameworks: the UK Corporate Governance Code, FTSE Women Leaders Review
(successor to the Hampton-Alexander Review), the Parker Review, EU CSRD/ESRS, and SEC
human capital and board diversity disclosure rules.

**Reference domain expert:** Dr Rita Goyal — Assistant Professor (Research), Centre for
Financial and Corporate Integrity, Coventry University. Research focus: board diversity, the
glass cliff phenomenon, women on boards, and strategic leadership. Brings 28 years of
industry experience (including 8 years as CFO), 70+ elite interviews, and an
industry-to-academia perspective.

## When to use

- A listed company preparing for **board refresh, refreshment, or succession**.
- A governance team responding to new **regulatory diversity disclosure requirements**.
- A board commissioning a **DEI policy** or strengthening its **nomination committee** process.
- An **investor stewardship** team benchmarking portfolio company board diversity.
- **Academic / consulting research** on board composition, the glass cliff, or board effectiveness.

## Usage

1. Load `wbs_template.json` into Agentic PMO to scaffold a new engagement.
2. Use `playbook.md` to brief the team and sequence phase gates.
3. Feed `agent_context.md` to the Plan, Monitor, and Alert agents.
4. Import `risk_register.json`, `monitoring_rules.json`, and `kpi_definitions.json` into the governance engine.
5. Use the two checklists during procurement and phase commissioning.

## Disclaimer

This pack is a planning aid, not legal, regulatory, or investment advice. Board diversity
obligations vary by jurisdiction, listing venue, and company size. Always validate against
the current corporate governance code, listing rules, and the company's articles of
association before adoption.
