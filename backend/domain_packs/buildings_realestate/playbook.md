# Phase-Gate Lifecycle Playbook — High-Rise Commercial Mixed-Use Tower

> **Note on duration:** Total programme duration for a 45–60 storey high-rise mixed-use tower is typically **36–60 months** end-to-end, dominated by permitting lead times (Phases 1–3), long-lead procurement (tower cranes, elevators, curtain wall), and the repetitive floor-cycle production period (Phases 5–6). Plans materially shorter than this range should be challenged by the Plan Agent.

## Phase 0: Strategic Framing

**Objective:** Establish the investment thesis, mixed-use brief (retail / office / residential / hotel), indicative massing and height, funding structure, and a realistic 36–60 month master programme before committing to acquisition.

**Key activities:**
- Define project vision and mixed-use tenancy mix targets
- Market demand study for the catchment (office absorption, residential pricing, retail footfall)
- Indicative massing and height feasibility against planning context
- Preliminary capital cost benchmarking (cost per sqm GFA for high-rise typology)
- Funding structure and investor alignment
- Stakeholder mapping including local authority / municipal planning authority
- High-level master programme with phase gates

**Exit Criteria:**
- Investment committee go/no-go decision documented
- Approved brief, indicative GFA, and height envelope
- Funded Phase 1 budget released
- Master programme baseline accepted

---

## Phase 1: Site Acquisition, Feasibility & Due Diligence

**Objective:** Confirm the site can physically, legally, and commercially support a tall tower with a deep basement; complete acquisition and de-risk ground conditions, contamination, neighbours, and planning appetite.

**Key activities:**
- Title, tenure, and legal due diligence; acquisition close
- Phase 1/2 ground investigation — boreholes, trial pits, groundwater monitoring
- Land contamination survey and preliminary remediation strategy
- Topographic and utility condition survey (existing services diversions scoping)
- Neighbouring building condition surveys and party-wall pre-assessment
- Planning pre-application consultation with the local authority / municipal planning authority
- EIA screening opinion
- Foundation and deep-basement feasibility (piled raft vs barrette piles; secant/contiguous walls)
- Tower crane strategy and urban site logistics concept
- Outline fire strategy concept for a tall building

**Exit Criteria:**
- Acquisition legally completed
- Geotechnical interpretive report issued with recommended foundation solution
- Remediation strategy endorsed by environmental regulator/local authority
- Planning pre-application feedback confirms deliverable envelope
- Feasibility-stage cost plan within funding tolerance

## Phase 2: Concept & Pre-Construction Design

**Objective:** Freeze the concept: floor plates, core position and size, structural scheme (core-and-slab with post-tensioned floors), facade concept, vertical transportation, MEP riser zoning, and outline fire strategy — sufficient to submit for planning and launch long-lead procurement.

**Key activities:**
- Concept architectural design — typical floor plates, core layout, vertical circulation
- Structural scheme — RC core with post-tensioned flat slabs; transfer structures at podium
- Foundation scheme — piling and basement retaining wall design basis
- Basement level count, excavation support, and dewatering concept
- Facade concept — unitised curtain wall vs stick system trade-off
- Vertical transportation study — elevator counts, speeds, zoning (low/mid/high rise)
- MEP riser zoning and plant space allocation per floor
- Outline fire strategy report (evacuation, firefighting cores, smoke control principles)
- Sustainability pre-assessment (BREEAM/LEED target setting)
- Planning submission drawing package
- Pre-construction cost plan
- Construction methodology statement — core jump cycles, tower crane climbing sequence
- Long-lead procurement register initiated (elevators, curtain wall, cranes, switchgear)

**Exit Criteria:**
- Stage gate design review passed — concept frozen
- Planning submission lodged
- Long-lead register approved with target order dates
- Cost plan signed off by developer-client

---

## Phase 3: Detailed Design, Approvals & Permitting

**Objective:** Complete technical design to construction-issue standard and secure all statutory approvals: full planning permission, building regulations/building control approval, fire strategy approval, party-wall agreements, highway/oversail licences, and discharge consents.

**Key activities:**
- Detailed architectural design development
- Detailed structural design — post-tensioned slab design, punching shear, transfer structures
- Piling and basement wall detailed design; temporary works excavation design certification
- Structural steel connection design for steel-frame elements
- Detailed facade engineering — thermal, structural, acoustic glazing calculations
- Detailed fire strategy and smoke control design
- MEP detailed design — risers, plant rooms, builder's work interfaces
- Building control / building regulations submission and approvals
- Full planning permission determination and condition discharge
- Party-wall agreements executed with neighbouring owners
- Highway/oversail, hoarding, and street works licences
- Dewatering and water discharge permit
- Detailed design stage gate sign-off (federated BIM model clash-free)

**Exit Criteria:**
- Full planning permission granted; pre-commencement conditions discharged
- Building control approval for structural and fire elements obtained
- Party-wall agreements signed with all affected neighbours
- Federated model clash-free at LOD 350+
- Construction-issue drawing register released

---

## Phase 4: Procurement & Subcontractor Award

**Objective:** Award all critical packages — main contractor, tower cranes, piling, structural steel, post-tensioning, self-climbing formwork, curtain wall, elevators, MEP — with contracts, bonds, and guarantees executed, aligned to the construction sequence.

**Key activities:**
- Main contractor procurement strategy and tender award
- Tower crane package tender and award (climbing/flat-top, climbing schedule)
- Piling subcontractor award
- Structural steel fabricator award
- Post-tensioning specialist award
- Formwork/falsework and self-climbing core system award
- Curtain wall fabricator and installer award (mock-up commitment included)
- Elevator/escalator supplier award — long-lead order placement
- MEP package subcontractor award
- Ready-mix concrete supply framework (multiple plants, continuity plan)
- Cladding panel supplier award
- Basement waterproofing specialist award
- Insurance, performance guarantees, and retention terms reviewed
- Contract closeout — all awards executed

**Exit Criteria:**
- 100% of critical-path packages awarded with executed contracts
- Long-lead orders placed (elevators, curtain wall, cranes) with confirmed delivery dates
- Performance security/bonds in place
- Procurement log reconciled to cost plan

---

## Phase 5: Construction — Enabling, Substructure & Core

**Objective:** Mobilise the site, complete remediation and piling, excavate and construct the deep basement, cast the transfer/ground-floor structure, and establish the repetitive core jump and post-tensioned slab production rhythm.

**Key activities:**
- Site mobilisation — hoarding, welfare, logistics, crane base and first tower crane erection
- Land remediation and contaminated soil removal
- Secant/contiguous piling to basement perimeter and foundations; pile integrity testing
- Bulk excavation with dewatering; propping/anchoring of basement walls
- Basement waterproofing/tanking installation and protection
- Raft and base slab pours; basement concrete frame construction level by level
- Ground-floor slab and transfer structure (structural steel + concrete composite where applicable)
- Core jump commencement — self-climbing formwork cycle established
- Tower crane climbing sequence operations begin
- First post-tensioned typical floor slabs — install, stress, grout
- Slab cycle optimisation toward target floor cycle time
- Substructure completion gate — waterproofing integrity verified

**Exit Criteria:**
- Piling complete with 100% integrity test pass
- Basement watertight — no active ingress; tanking certified
- Transfer structure complete and inspected
- Core jump cycle stabilised at planned duration
- Zero lost-time incidents during excavation phase

---

## Phase 6: Construction — Superstructure, Facade & Fit-Out

**Objective:** Drive the repetitive superstructure to roof level, follow with curtain wall/cladding close-in, MEP riser installation, elevator installation, and internal fit-out; dismantle tower cranes and strike temporary works.

**Key activities:**
- Repetitive concrete frame floor cycles (form, pour, stress PT, strip)
- Core jump progression tracked to roof level
- Post-tensioned slab stressing and grouting QA on every floor
- Tower crane climb-ups, tie-ins, and final dismantle/removal
- Curtain wall unit delivery and floor-by-floor installation behind the frame
- Facade zone air/water infiltration testing as zones close
- Cladding panel installation to podium and amenity areas
- MEP riser installation and vertical continuity through all levels
- MEP riser coordination clash resolution in live model
- Elevator shaft handover, rails, machines, car installation and testing
- Internal fit-out — drywall, ceilings, flooring, second fix
- Facade close-in and slab-to-facade void sealing/fire stopping
- Roof plant installation; crane dismantle preparation
- Perimeter scaffolding strike and external works commencement

**Exit Criteria:**
- Structure topped out; core complete to roof
- Facade weathertight — infiltration tests passed per zone
- MEP risers pressure-tested and continuous top-to-bottom
- Elevators operational for construction/handover use
- Tower cranes removed; site cleared of major temporary works

---

## Phase 7: Commissioning, Handover & Asset Operations

**Objective:** Prove every life-safety and building system, obtain the building control completion certificate, complete snagging, handover stratified assets (retail, office, residential) to operators, and enter the defects liability period.

**Key activities:**
- MEP pre-commissioning — flushing, pressure tests, electrical IR tests
- Fire alarm cause & effect testing; sprinkler hydrostatic testing
- Smoke control and stair pressurisation testing
- Elevator/escalator commissioning including safe-mode wet tests
- BMS point-to-point commissioning and integration
- Emergency lighting duration tests
- Cooling/heating plant performance commissioning
- Building control final inspection and completion certificate
- Snagging and handover walkdown with client and operators
- O&M manuals, as-built drawings, and asset data (COBie/CAFM) handover
- Soft landings support and defects liability period mobilisation

**Exit Criteria:**
- Completion certificate issued by building control body
- All life-safety systems witnessed and certified
- Practical completion certified per stratified unit/tenancy
- Handover documentation pack accepted by operator
- DLP regime active with named defect management owner
