# SAF Biomass Gasification FT — Phase-Gate Project Playbook

## Project Lifecycle Overview

This playbook defines an 8-phase phase-gate delivery framework for a Sustainable Aviation Fuel (SAF) facility using biomass gasification followed by Fischer-Tropsch synthesis (FT-SPK). Each phase has clear objectives, deliverables, and gate criteria.

**Typical duration:** 36–54 months from concept to first commercial SAF production.

---

## Phase 0: Strategic Framing and Investment Case

**Objective:** Establish strategic rationale, target market, high-level economics, and investment thesis.

Key activities:
- Strategic positioning within aviation decarbonisation / CORSIA / ReFuelEU / SAF mandates
- Target market analysis (domestic vs international, blending mandates, offtake demand)
- Feedstock availability at macro level (regionally available biomass types, volumes, seasonality)
- Preliminary technology assessment (gasification + FT pathway vs HEFA, ATJ, Power-to-Liquid)
- Class 5 CAPEX/OPEX estimate (±50%)
- Project structure (SPV, JV, technology partner)
- Investment memo and board/stakeholder approval

**Gate 0 → 1:** Strategic alignment confirmed; investment thesis approved; project budget allocated for Phase 1 study.

---

## Phase 1: Feedstock, Site, and Market Feasibility

**Objective:** Validate feedstock supply, site suitability, and market offtake at feasibility level.

Key activities:
- Detailed feedstock assessment (types: forestry residues, agri residues, energy crops, MSW fractions)
- Feedstock sampling and characterisation (moisture, ash, ultimate/proximate analysis, HHV, contaminants)
- Feedstock supply chain design (collection, transport, storage, seasonality management)
- Moisture/ash/contaminant management strategy (drying, blending, pre-treatment)
- Site selection (multi-criteria: logistics, utilities, feedstock radius, product offtake, community)
- Geotechnical survey, topographic survey, environmental baseline
- Market study: SAF demand, offtake partnerships, blending mandates, co-product markets (naphtha, diesel, wax)
- Preliminary product distribution logistics (pipeline, truck, rail, airport fuel terminal interface)
- Techno-economic assessment (TEA) and LCA carbon intensity estimate (GREET / RSB)

**Gate 1 → 2:** Feedstock confirmed viable; short-listed site(s) identified; offtake interest confirmed; proceed to technology selection.

---

## Phase 2: Technology Selection and Pre-FEED

**Objective:** Downselect gasification technology, syngas cleanup train, FT synthesis configuration, and hydroprocessing package. Produce pre-FEED package to ±30% cost accuracy.

Key activities:
- Gasifier technology evaluation (fluidised bed, entrained flow, updraft/downdraft fixed bed)
- Gasification oxidant selection (air, oxygen, oxygen-enriched air, steam)
- Syngas cleanup technology selection (hot/cold gas cleanup, tar reforming/cracking, particulate removal, acid gas removal)
- CO2 removal strategy (PSA, amine, membrane)
- Water-gas shift / H2/CO ratio adjustment (sour shift, sweet shift, membrane)
- FT catalyst and reactor selection (cobalt vs iron; slurry bubble column vs fixed bed vs microchannel)
- Wax handling and hydrocracking/isomerization technology selection
- Hydroprocessing severity and product slate optimisation (max jet vs max diesel)
- Hydrogen supply strategy (SMR, ATR, electrolysis, recycle)
- Product fractionation scheme (naphtha, jet, diesel, wax, offgas)
- Pre-FEED mass and energy balance
- Process flow diagrams (PFDs)
- Preliminary plot plan and equipment list
- Preliminary HAZID
- Technology licensor selection and term sheet

**Gate 2 → 3:** Technology route selected; technology licence(s) agreed in principle; Pre-FEED cost ±30%; proceed to FEED.

---

## Phase 3: FEED, Permitting, and Certification Planning

**Objective:** Complete Front-End Engineering and Design (FEED), secure major permits, and initiate ASTM / sustainability certification pathways.

Key activities:
- FEED engineering: P&IDs, PFDs, equipment datasheets, line list, valve list, insulation spec
- Process engineering: heat and material balance (HMB), utility balance, flare load
- Detailed plot plan and 3D model (30%)
- EPC cost estimate (Class 3 ±15%)
- HAZOP and SIL/LOPA studies
- Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) and Environmental Clearance (EC)
- Air emissions modelling and consent application
- Water abstraction, discharge, and treatment permits
- Hazardous chemical storage and processing licence (PESO / equivalent)
- Fire safety NOC and building plan approval
- ASTM D7566 FT-SPK certification pathway planning
- Sustainability certification scheme selection (CORSIA, ISCC EU, RSB)
- Chain-of-custody documentation system design
- Offtake term sheet and logistics agreement negotiation

**Gate 3 → 4:** FEED complete; major permits submitted; EPC estimate within ±15%; ASTM and sustainability plan established; proceed to EPC.

---

## Phase 4: EPC Procurement and Construction

**Objective:** Procure all long-lead and critical equipment; award EPC contract(s); execute site construction.

Key activities:
- EPC contract award (lump-sum turnkey / EPCM / multiple packages)
- Long-lead procurement: gasifier, syngas cleanup, FT reactor, hydrocracker, compressors, columns
- FT catalyst order and delivery schedule
- Site preparation, earthworks, foundations
- Civil and structural construction
- Mechanical equipment installation
- Piping fabrication and erection
- Electrical and instrumentation installation
- DCS / SIS / fire & gas system installation
- Insulation, painting, fireproofing
- Quality management: NDT, inspection, certification
- Project controls: schedule, cost, change management
- Permit-to-work (PTW) and construction HSE

**Gate 4 → 5:** Construction mechanical completion; system walk-down complete; punch list generated; ready for pre-commissioning.

---

## Phase 5: Pre-Commissioning and Commissioning Readiness

**Objective:** Complete all pre-commissioning activities and confirm readiness for safe commissioning and start-up.

Key activities:
- System/sub-system turnover from construction to commissioning team
- Hydrostatic and pneumatic leak testing of all pressure systems
- Instrument loop checks and DCS signal verification
- Electrical: megger testing, motor bump, rotation check
- Piping: flushing, chemical cleaning, steam blowing
- Refractory dry-out (gasifier, reformer if present)
- Relief valve setting and flare system blow-down test
- Dry commissioning of rotating equipment (compressors, pumps, fans)
- Control logic testing: DCS, SIS, ESD, fire & gas
- Commissioning team training and simulator validation
- Operating procedures, start-up manuals, emergency procedures finalised
- HAZOP recommendations close-out verification
- Commissioning readiness review (CRR)
- Operator training and competency assessment

**Gate 5 → 6:** Pre-commissioning complete; all safety systems verified; commissioning team ready; regulator inspections passed.

---

## Phase 6: Start-up, Product Qualification, and ASTM Pathway Compliance

**Objective:** Commission and start up each unit in sequence; produce first SAF; qualify product to ASTM D7566 Annex A1; pass performance test.

Key activities:
- Utility systems start-up (cooling water, steam, nitrogen, instrument air)
- Feedstock handling and drying system commissioning
- Gasifier cold commissioning (air/nitrogen circulation, refractory heat-up)
- Gasifier hot commissioning (feedstock introduction, syngas production)
- Syngas cleanup commissioning (particulate removal, tar reforming, acid gas removal)
- Guard bed loading and commissioning
- FT catalyst loading and in-situ activation (reduction)
- FT loop pressurisation and syngas introduction
- FT reaction ramp-up to design conversion
- Wax handling and hydrocracker catalyst loading/activation
- Hydrocracker start-up and severity adjustment
- Fractionation commissioning (naphtha, jet, diesel, wax cuts)
- Product sampling and laboratory analysis
- ASTM D7566 FT-SPK fuel property testing (freeze point, flash point, density, aromatics, T90/T95, etc.)
- Blending trial with conventional Jet A-1 (up to 50% FT-SPK)
- 72-hour performance guarantee test run
- Product qualification package and ASTM certificate of analysis
- Sustainability audit and certification body verification
- First commercial SAF delivery

**Gate 6 → 7:** Performance test passed; ASTM D7566 FT-SPK qualification complete; first production at nameplate; proceed to operations.

---

## Phase 7: Operations Handover, Optimization, and Sustainability Reporting

**Objective:** Hand over fully verified plant to operations team; optimise performance; establish ongoing sustainability reporting.

Key activities:
- System turnover: operations team assumes full responsibility
- Punch list close-out and final completion certificate
- As-built documentation handover (P&IDs, O&M manuals, equipment files)
- Spare parts inventory and maintenance planning system handover
- Operating parameters optimisation (catalyst performance, yield optimisation, energy efficiency)
- Catalyst performance monitoring and regeneration schedule
- Carbon intensity monitoring and sustainability reporting (annual)
- ASTM and sustainability certification maintenance (annual audits)
- Offtake logistics steady-state operation
- Operational KPIs and dashboard establishment
- Continuous improvement programme (yield, reliability, safety)
- Lessons learned and project close-out report

**Gate 7 → Steady Operations:** Plant in commercial operation; all warranties closed; sustainability reporting cycle established.

## Technical Process Notes

### Biomass Feedstock
Typical feedstocks: forestry residues (wood chips, bark), agricultural residues (straw, corn stover, bagasse), energy crops (miscanthus, switchgrass), MSW biomass fraction. Critical parameters: moisture (target <15-20 wt%), ash (target <5 wt%), particle size, alkali/chlorine/sulfur content.

### Gasification
Gasification converts biomass at 700-1400°C with controlled oxidant (air/oxygen/steam) into raw syngas. Key gasifier types: circulating fluidised bed (CFB), bubbling fluidised bed (BFB), entrained flow, indirect (dual-bed). Syngas composition varies: CO 15-50%, H2 10-40%, CO2 5-25%, CH4 2-15%, plus N2 if air-blown.

### Syngas Cleanup & Conditioning
Raw syngas contains: particulates (ash, char), tars, sulfur (H2S, COS, mercaptans), chlorine (HCl), alkali metals, ammonia. FT catalysts (especially cobalt) are highly sensitive to sulfur (<10 ppbv) and chlorine. Cleanup train typically: cyclone → filter → tar reformer → water quench → acid gas removal → guard beds. H2/CO ratio for cobalt FT: ~1.8-2.2:1; for iron FT: ~0.6-1.5:1 (WGS-active). Conditioning via water-gas shift or membrane.

### Fischer-Tropsch Synthesis
FT converts syngas to paraffinic hydrocarbons via:
- Cobalt catalyst: high activity, low WGS, high wax selectivity (preferred for jet)
- Iron catalyst: lower cost, WGS-active, higher olefin/oxygenate content
Reactor types: slurry bubble column (preferred for large scale), fixed bed (multi-tubular), microchannel (compact).
Typical conditions: 200-250°C (LTFT for max wax/jet), 20-40 bar.

### Hydrocracking & Isomerization
FT wax (C20+) is hydrocracked/isomerized to maximise jet-range (C9-C15) paraffins and improve cold-flow properties. Two-stage or single-stage hydrocracking with precious metal (Pt/Pd) or base metal catalyst.

### Product Fractionation
Separation train: deethanizer/depropanizer → naphtha splitter → jet column → diesel column → wax column. Offgas (C1-C3) sent to fuel gas or recycle. Water co-product purified for recycle or treatment.

### ASTM D7566 Annex A1 (FT-SPK)
Maximum blend ratio: 50% FT-SPK with conventional Jet A-1. Key property requirements: freeze point ≤ -47°C, flash point ≥ 38°C, density 730-770 kg/m³, T90 ≤ 300°C, aromatics 8-25 vol%, sulfur ≤ 15 ppm, thermal stability at 325°C (JFTOT).
