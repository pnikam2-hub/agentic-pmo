# Green Methanol via Biomass Gasification — Project Playbook

## Overview

This playbook describes the project lifecycle for a **green methanol production facility** using the **biomass gasification pathway**. The core chemical route is:

```
Biomass → Gasification → Syngas Cleanup → Syngas Ratio Adjustment → Methanol Synthesis → Purification → Green Methanol
```

Green methanol produced via this pathway is a drop-in replacement for fossil methanol, used as a marine fuel, chemical feedstock, or hydrogen carrier. The carbon in the biomass is biogenic, making the fuel carbon-negative or carbon-neutral depending on feedstock origin and process energy source.

---

## Phase-Gate Structure

### Phase 0: Strategic Framing
**Gate: FEL-1 approval**

Define project scope, business case, feedstock strategy, and product offtake.

- Market analysis for green methanol (marine fuel, chemical, blending)
- Feedstock availability assessment: agricultural residue, forestry residue, organic waste
- Preliminary mass balance and carbon intensity estimate
- Technology landscape review: gasifier types, methanol synthesis options
- Partner/joint venture strategy (feedstock suppliers, technology licensors, offtakers)
- High-level CAPEX/OPEX estimate (±50%)
- Project charter and team definition
- Preliminary schedule and phase budget

### Phase 1: Feedstock and Site Feasibility
**Gate: FEL-2 approval**

Confirm feedstock security and identify preferred site.

- Detailed feedstock supply study: type, volume, seasonality, logistics, cost curve
- Biomass characterisation: moisture, ash, volatiles, ultimate analysis, alkali metals, chlorine
- Site selection: proximity to feedstock, water, power, grid, port, product offtake
- Environmental baseline study
- Feedstock handling and storage concept: covered storage, drying, sizing, blending
- Feedstock supply agreement heads of terms
- Preliminary plot plan and layout
- Technology pre-screening: gasifier vendors, methanol licensors

### Phase 2: Technology Selection and Pre-FEED
**Gate: FEL-3 approval**

Select core technology and produce ±30% cost estimate.

- Gasifier selection: fixed bed, fluidised bed, entrained flow, plasma/advanced
- Oxidant selection: air-blown, oxygen-blown, steam, oxygen-steam
- Syngas cleanup technology: tar reformer, wet scrubber, filter, acid gas removal
- Methanol synthesis reactor configuration: boiling water reactor, multi-bed, quench
- Distillation configuration: two-column or three-column
- Mass and energy balance (process simulation)
- Preliminary P&IDs and heat integration
- Utility requirements: power, steam, cooling water, nitrogen, instrument air
- CAPEX estimate ±30% and OPEX estimate
- Technology licensor selection and license terms negotiation
- EPC contracting strategy
- Feasibility study report

### Phase 3: FEED and Permitting
**Gate: Investment decision / FID**

Produce detailed engineering basis and secure all major permits.

- Front-end engineering design (FEED)
- Detailed P&IDs, plot plan, line list, equipment list
- HAZID/HAZOP studies
- Quantitative risk assessment (QRA)
- Environmental impact assessment (EIA)
- Key permits: environmental clearance, air emissions, hazardous chemicals, methanol storage
- Fire safety approval, factory licence
- Biomass transport and storage permits
- Ash/char disposal authorisation
- Land acquisition and zoning
- Technology license agreement execution
- EPC tender package preparation
- Long-lead equipment ordering (gasifier, compressor, methanol reactor, distillation columns)
- Offtake agreement execution
- Financing close

### Phase 4: EPC Procurement and Construction
**Gate: Mechanical completion**

Procure equipment, construct the plant.

- EPC contract award
- Detailed engineering (issue for construction)
- Procure: biomass handling, gasifier, syngas cleanup, compressors, methanol reactor, distillation, DCS, flare
- Vendor document review and approval
- Civil works: foundations, roads, drains, buildings
- Structural steel fabrication and erection
- Equipment setting and alignment
- Piping fabrication and erection
- Electrical and instrumentation installation
- Catalyst procurement and storage
- Pre-commissioning procedure preparation
- Quality assurance inspections
- Progress monitoring and reporting
- Change order management

### Phase 5: Pre-Commissioning
**Gate: Pre-commissioning complete**

Verify all systems are mechanically complete and safe to start.

- Mechanical completion checks per system
- Punch list items resolved
- Piping hydrotest, flushing, and chemical cleaning
- Instrument loop checks
- Electrical megger checks and motor run-in
- Control system (DCS/SCADA) checkout
- Refractory dry-out for gasifier
- Catalyst loading (methanol synthesis reactor)
- Dry-run of biomass handling system
- Safety system function tests (ESD, fire & gas)
- Flare system check and purging
- Pre-start-up safety review (PSSR)
- Operations team training and certification
- Standard operating procedure (SOP) validation

### Phase 6: Commissioning and Performance Testing
**Gate: Interim acceptance certificate**

Introduce feedstocks and verify design performance.

- Biomass feeding trials (graduated rate)
- Gasifier light-up and refractory heat-up
- Syngas production at turndown → design rate
- Syngas cleanup system validation (tar, particulate, sulfur, chlorine removal)
- Methanol synthesis loop start-up (catalyst reduction, loop pressurisation)
- Crude methanol production
- Distillation start-up and product quality adjustment
- Product methanol loading to storage
- Performance guarantee test run (72–100 hours at nameplate capacity)
- Product quality certification (IMPCA specification)
- Emissions compliance testing
- By-product management (ash/char, wastewater, off-gas)
- Performance test report
- Interim acceptance certificate (IAC)

### Phase 7: Operations Handover and Optimisation
**Gate: Final acceptance certificate**

Transfer to operations team; achieve steady-state commercial operation.

- Operations and maintenance manual handover
- Spare parts inventory and warehouse transfer
- Reliability and availability tracking
- Catalyst performance monitoring
- Feedstock optimisation (blending, moisture control)
- Syngas ratio fine-tuning
- Energy efficiency improvement
- Environmental compliance reporting
- Warranty claims management
- Operator training completion
- Final acceptance certificate (FAC)
- Lessons learned report
- Continuous improvement program launch

## Key Technical Considerations

| Area | Critical Issue | Why It Matters |
|------|---------------|----------------|
| Feedstock | Moisture content (>30% impacts gasifier efficiency) | Affects syngas quality, energy balance, drying cost |
| Feedstock | Ash content and composition (slagging/fouling) | Can damage refractory, reduce gasifier uptime |
| Gasification | Tar formation | Plug lines, damage scrubbers, reduce syngas yield |
| Syngas Cleanup | Catalyst poisons (sulfur, chlorine, alkali metals) | Destroys methanol catalyst irreversibly |
| Ratio Adjustment | H2/CO ratio below 2 | Reduces methanol conversion, increases recycle |
| Methanol Synthesis | Copper catalyst deactivation | Directly impacts methanol output and cost |
| Purification | Methanol-water azeotrope | Requires distillation for fuel-grade methanol |
