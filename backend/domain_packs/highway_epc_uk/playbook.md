# Playbook — Highway EPC (UK) phase-gate lifecycle

PMO/domain reference — must be reviewed by qualified highway/structural engineers.

This playbook narrates the eight-phase lifecycle used by the `highway_epc_uk` domain pack. Each phase has a **gate** (the decision that releases the next phase), typical **activities**, **outputs** and the UK-specific standards or consents in play. Phase numbers map to the `phase` integer on each WBS task (0–7).

---

## Phase 0 — Strategic Framing

**Objective.** Establish the strategic need, the funding envelope and the delivery model for a scheme on the Strategic Road Network (SRN).

**Activities.** Scheme identification and need case; stakeholder mapping; outline business case; corridor/study area definition; baseline traffic-data scope; EPC/delivery-model framing; CDM 2015 duty-holder appointments; common data environment (CDE/BIM) set-up; initial master schedule.

**Gate 0 — Endorsement to proceed to feasibility.** Sponsor endorses the strategic need and funding envelope; CDM duty-holders appointed; DMRB Stage 1 entry criteria confirmed.

**Key outputs.** Strategic Outline Statement; stakeholder & engagement plan; initial business case; DMRB Stage 1 entry log.

---

## Phase 1 — Feasibility & Option Assessment (DMRB Stage 2)

**Objective.** Appraise route options and recommend a preferred option against transport, economic, environmental and cost criteria.

**Activities.** Traffic data collection and baseline surveys; DMRB Stage 2 option assessment; route-option development (online/offline); preliminary traffic forecasting and economic appraisal; initial ground-investigation scope; environmental scoping (EIA screening); non-statutory public consultation; option costing & value engineering; consent-route determination (DCO/TWAO/Planning).

**Gate 1 — Preferred option endorsed.** Preferred route announced (PRA) following DMRB Stage 2 recommendation and sponsor endorsement; benefit–cost ratio acceptable within the funding envelope.

**Key outputs.** DMRB Stage 2 report; economic appraisal; EIA scoping report; option cost estimates; preferred option recommendation.

---

## Phase 2 — Scheme Design & Pre-Construction (Preliminary Design / DMRB Stage 3)

**Objective.** Develop the preferred option to a fixed preliminary design suitable for statutory consultation and consent submission.

**Activities.** DMRB Stage 3 scheme assessment; preliminary horizontal/vertical alignment; outline pavement design; structures feasibility (overbridge/underbridge replacement); drainage strategy to CD 527; geotechnical ground model; EIA chapters; ecology surveys (bats, badgers, GCN, birds); archaeology and heritage; noise & air quality; Stage 1 Road Safety Audit; statutory consultation and Design Fix 1.

**Gate 2 — Scheme assessment accepted; design frozen for consent.** Stage 3 assessment accepted, Stage 1 RSA recommendations closed out, Design Fix 1 issued.

**Key outputs.** DMRB Stage 3 report; preliminary design package; draft EIA chapters; Stage 1 RSA report; Design Fix 1 pack.

---

## Phase 3 — Detailed Design & Consents (DCO/TWAO, EIA, Public Inquiry, CPO/land)

**Objective.** Complete detailed design, obtain development consent, secure land possession and agree diversions/statutory approvals.

**Activities.** Detailed pavement design (CD 236); structures detailed design with Category 3 check; detailed drainage & SUDS; smart-motorway technology design (MIDAS/ERF/HSR); lighting to BS 5489; signs, gantries & VMS design; EIA submission; Draft Order Publication (DCO/TWAO); statutory consultation; Public Inquiry/Examination; DCO determination; land acquisition/CPO; utility-diversion design; Section 278 / street-works agreements; Stage 2 RSA; ecology mitigation licences (Natural England).

**Gate 3 — Consent obtained and land available.** DCO/TWAO made and sealed; CPO land vested/possessed; utility diversions and S278 agreements in place; Stage 2 RSA closed out; mitigation licences granted.

**Key outputs.** Detailed design packages; DCO grant; land/CPO schedule; diversion designs; S278 agreements; Stage 2 RSA report; mitigation licences.

> **UK note:** NSIPs require a Development Consent Order. A Public Inquiry or Examination in Public can extend this phase by many months; land acquisition and utility diversions are frequent critical-path items.

---

## Phase 4 — Procurement & Construction Preparation

**Objective.** Procure long-lead and main-works packages and prepare the site and method of construction.

**Activities.** Procurement strategy and package schedule; tender & prequalification; long-lead procurement (structural steel, precast, gantries); main-works contract award & mobilisation; site establishment/compounds; traffic-management staging plans; construction sequence & method statements; CDM construction-phase plan; permits to dig & street-works permits; night-closures/lane-rental scheme; enabling works; materials management plan.

**Gate 4 — Construction release.** Main-works contract awarded; CDM construction-phase plan accepted; TM staging plans accepted; long-lead orders placed against the programme; site established.

**Key outputs.** Executed main-works contract; long-lead orders; construction-phase plan; TM staging plans; permits register.

---

## Phase 5 — Construction Execution (main works, TM, night closures)

**Objective.** Build the works to the detailed design, safely, under traffic management and within possession windows.

**Activities.** Construction-phase ground investigation; bulk earthworks and import/export balance; earthworks compaction & testing; pavement sub-base, base and binder; structures foundations & piling; concrete & steel bridge works; drainage installation; road restraint systems (VRS); gantry foundations & erection; smart-motorway technology installation; signs/VMS; lighting & electrical; final surfacing & white lining; landscaping & environmental planting; traffic management execution; night closures & lane rental; seasonal/weather management.

**Gate 5 — Substantial completion ready for commissioning.** Works complete to the point where systems can be energised and tested; Stage 2 RSA actions closed; defects/snagging list opened.

**Key outputs.** Built works; piling & materials records; TM operations log; closure logs; quality records.

> **UK note:** Most works are delivered under narrow possession windows (often overnight) under a lane-rental scheme. Surfacing is weather-sensitive; missed weather windows and utility clashes with TM phasing are common risks.

---

## Phase 6 — Commissioning & Performance Testing (incl. safety audits)

**Objective.** Prove and energise all systems and pass performance and safety checks.

**Activities.** Structural bearings & joints commissioning; drainage outfall proving; asphalt compaction validation; electrical lighting energisation into operation; smart-motorway technology integration test; VRS impact certification & acceptance; traffic trial load; Stage 3 Road Safety Audit (DMRB CD 377); systems integration & communications test; pre-commissioning checks & snagging.

**Gate 6 — Ready for handover.** All commissioning tests passed; Stage 3 RSA recommendations closed out; systems operational.

**Key outputs.** Commissioning records; integration test reports; VRS certificate; Stage 3 RSA report.

---

## Phase 7 — Completion, Handover & Optimisation

**Objective.** Close out snags, hand over to the maintainer and evaluate performance.

**Activities.** Category 3 corrections/snagging; final snagging walkdown with National Highways; spatial safety audit; completion certificate & as-built handover; operations & maintenance manuals; post-opening project evaluation (POPE); defects liability period management; lessons learned & close-out.

**Gate 7 — Project closed.** Completion certificate issued; as-built record and O&M manuals accepted; POPE submitted; defects liability underway.

**Key outputs.** Completion certificate; as-built record; O&M manuals; POPE report; close-out report.

---

## Cross-phase disciplines

PMO, Civil/Structural, Electrical Engineering, Instrumentation/Controls (ITS/smart motorway), Construction, Procurement, Commissioning, Quality, HSE, Permitting, Document Control and Project Controls run across all phases. Monitoring cadence is **weekly** for construction progress and **monthly** for cost, risk and KPI reviews (see `agent_context.md`).
